Featured projects

Does Visual Grounding Help a Small Language Model Learn More Efficiently?

Controlled pretraining study under BabyLM Challenge 2026 constraints: GPT-2 from scratch, text-only vs visually grounded, with architecture and word budget held fixed. Evaluated on perplexity, BLiMP (67 grammatical paradigms) and EWoK.

Finding: grounding made the model a better next-word predictor but not a better grammarian. Perplexity fell 39% while BLiMP accuracy dropped slightly but significantly.

Perplexity ↓ 39%  ·  BLiMP 60.43% → 58.46% (p=0.001)
PythonPyTorchTransformersDINOv2

Before the Fact-Check: Detecting Misinformation Through Linguistic Style

DistilBERT + XGBoost on interpretable linguistic features, trained on LIAR and stress-tested zero-shot on FakeNewsNet PolitiFact with SHAP analysis.

Finding: style cues that work on LIAR did not carry over to PolitiFact news. SHAP showed the model relying on dataset-specific patterns, a deployability gap that benchmark-only testing would have hidden.

In-domain F1 0.673  ·  Cross-domain F1 0.000
